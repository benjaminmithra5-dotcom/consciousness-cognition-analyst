Frequently Asked Questions

About the conversations

What is a Mind Interpreter?

A Mind Interpreter doesn't read minds. Through listening and questions, I help untangle your thoughts and bring you real clarity. You do the talking, and I help you put what's going on inside into words you can understand.

What can I talk about?

Anything at all. Some people come with something heavy, and some just need to say something out loud for the first time. For example:

  • The thought that keeps you awake at 3 a.m.
  • Feeling stuck, in a job, a relationship, or your own head
  • Something you can't say to your family, friends or partner
  • A decision you keep going back and forth on
  • Guilt, regret, or anger you've been carrying quietly
  • Loneliness, even when you're surrounded by people
  • A strange dream, a coincidence, or an experience you can't explain
  • A fantasy, a daydream, or a "what if" life you've imagined but never told anyone
  • A big idea, a question about life, or something you're just curious about
  • Good news or excitement that nobody around you seems to share

There's no topic too small, too strange, or too personal. If it's on your mind, it belongs here.

Do I need to prepare anything?

No. You don't need to know where the conversation should go. A few words to start are enough.

How is this different from therapy?

These conversations aren't therapy, counselling, or medical or psychological treatment, and I don't diagnose or treat any condition. They're a space to be heard and to think clearly. If you're seeing a therapist, this can sit alongside that, but it isn't a replacement.

How is it different from talking to a friend?

A friend often has opinions, history with you, or advice to give. Here, there's no judgment, no unasked-for advice, and nothing you say affects anyone in your life.

Will you tell me what to do?

No. I ask questions and help you see your own thoughts more clearly. The decisions are always yours.

Sessions and booking

How long is a session?

60 minutes.

How do sessions happen?

By video or voice call, on an app we agree on beforehand. You can keep your camera off if you prefer.

I'm not in India. Can we still talk?

Yes. Tell me your time zone and I'll find a time that works for you.

How do I book?

Send me a message on WhatsApp, by email, or through the Write to Me form, and we'll choose a time together.

How do I pay?

We'll go over payment options during our call, so you can choose what works best for you.

Can I reschedule or cancel?

Yes, just let me know 24 hours before.

Who are sessions for?

Sessions are for adults aged 18 and over.

What language are sessions in?

English only.

Privacy and safety

Is what I say kept private?

Yes. What you share stays private and is never shared with anyone, except where the law requires it or where someone's immediate safety is at serious risk.

What happens to my contact details?

Your messages and contact details are only used to arrange and hold our conversations. They're never sold, shared, or added to any mailing list, and you can ask for them to be deleted at any time.

What if I'm in crisis?

Please don't wait for a session. Contact your local emergency services or a crisis line right away (for example, 988 in the US).

The website

Are the Brain Games and Mind Exercises free?

Yes. Everything on the site is free to use, with no sign-up.

Do I need an account?

No. There are no accounts, logins, cookies or tracking on this site.

Are my game scores or answers saved?

No. Nothing you type or play is stored or sent anywhere.

What do the Brain Games train?

Memory, attention and thinking: Chess for strategy, Recall and N Back for working memory, and Card, Word and Number Memory for recall.

What do the Mind Exercises do?

They train calm and focus: Mindfulness Breathing slows you down, Leetspeak Reading sharpens attention, Flow Type builds focus stamina, and Guilford's Test exercises creative thinking.

What is the RV Lab?

A space to practise remote viewing, an experimental exercise in perception. You sketch and describe a hidden target offline, then reveal the image. It's exploratory and experimental, not a scientifically proven method.
